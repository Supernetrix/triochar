Greenerth partners with smallholder communities to eradicate open-burning of agro-residues and restore invasive-species-infused landscapes by converting them into durable, high-quality biochar using Kon-Tiki pyrolysis. Beyond delivering high-integrity carbon removal, the project actively improves soil health, restores degraded land, creates thousands of employment opportunities and increases rural incomes across India creating a virtuous cycle where environmental restoration and farmer livelihoods reinforce one another.

Every batch is tracked end-to-end by Greenerth's robust MRV system, which combines IoT sensor hardware with AI/ML-driven monitoring to ensure transparent, tamper-resistant data across the production chain and is independently verified by Carbon Standards International against rigorous, science-based methods.

By converting a waste and fire-risk liability into a permanent carbon sink, Navachar offers organizations a combination of durable, verifiable carbon removal paired with measurable social and environmental co-benefits making it an ideal fit for buyers seeking impact beyond just carbon removal.
